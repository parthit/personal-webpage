## Why this prompt shows up so often

URL shorteners are a favorite interview topic because they touch storage, hashing, redirects, and scale without needing obscure domain knowledge.

At a high level you need to:

1. Accept a long URL and return a short code
2. Redirect `short.ly/{code}` to the original URL quickly
3. Keep collisions rare and lookups cheap

## High-level design

![High-level URL shortener diagram](/content/images/writing/demo-url-shortener/diagram.svg)

A practical first cut:

- **API service** — creates short links and serves redirects
- **Datastore** — maps `code → long_url` (plus metadata)
- **Cache** — absorbs hot redirect traffic
- **ID / hash strategy** — base62 encoding of an ID, or a truncated hash with collision checks
